Design case study

How this website was designed, by Adewumi Adedotun Emmanuel, UI/UX designer.

The problem

Many furniture websites are difficult to navigate. Customers struggle to find a piece, see its price and know how to order.

Research

I reviewed other furniture websites and found the same issue again and again: confusing navigation. That became the main rule for this design.

Design decisions

  • Only three screens: Showroom, Collection and Order, so each step is one tap.
  • A price on every product, with filters and sorting to find a piece fast.
  • A 3D viewer with an exploded view, so customers can see how a piece is built.
  • Ordering through WhatsApp, email or a short form.
